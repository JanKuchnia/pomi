- No social media presence, customer-facing marketing language, or review-reply behavior was found anywhere — there is no visible "voice" beyond the one About-page paragraph and product line name ("STEEL GARDEN"). This is a genuinely thin tone signal; the brand-voice work at the next phase will have very little existing material to anchor on and may need to rely heavily on the checkpoint conversation with the client rather than inferred site tone.
- By contrast, competitor MAAN uses trust-building, longevity-forward language ("godny zaufania producent," 30+ years), and Romanik leans on heritage/tradition language (1989 roots, unchanged process).
